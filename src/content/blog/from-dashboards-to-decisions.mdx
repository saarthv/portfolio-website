Most analytics work starts with reporting but creates value only when it changes decisions.

## A simple progression

1. **Describe**: What happened?
2. **Diagnose**: Why did it happen?
3. **Decide**: What should happen next?

Teams often over-invest in the first step and under-invest in the third.

## What helps in practice

- A clear owner for each metric and threshold.
- Interfaces that make tradeoffs explicit, not hidden.
- Tight loops between model outputs and operating actions.

When product analytics is designed around these loops, dashboards become operating tools instead of passive views.
